How it works

Three streams of work. One composite map. Compliance built into every step.

A high-rise retrofit runs as up to three parallel streams. CAIELO tracks all of them on one live building map — and bakes the compliance checks directly into the workflow so nothing advances out of sequence.

A

Risers

Vertical · the building's spine

Replacement of the vertical water and chilled-water risers that run floor to floor. Pressure-tested and fire-stopped at every penetration.

B

In-Unit

Horizontal · inside occupied units

Kitec/PEX and FCU/heat-pump replacements inside residents' suites — the work that touches people's homes directly.

C

Headers

Corridors · the main arteries

The main mechanical headers, including welded black-steel under formal TSSA inspection — the highest-compliance work on the project.

The MEP dependency lock keeps streams in order. In-unit branch work stays locked until the riser or header serving it has passed inspection — so the platform mirrors the physical reality of the building, automatically.
